BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Remove the reasons to hesitate.

Clear terms beat pressure. These are the questions that normally slow down a website decision.

Do I own the website?

On one-time builds, yes: the agreed project is handed over after final payment. Managed memberships are an ongoing service arrangement; ownership and handoff terms are confirmed before you start.

What does “updates included” actually mean?

Reasonable content, service, product and offer updates within the plan scope. A major redesign, new app-level feature or large new system is scoped separately instead of being hidden inside vague “unlimited” wording.

Are hosting and SSL included?

They are included in managed plans. One-time builds include deployment setup; ongoing hosting terms are confirmed based on the project.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?

No credible website studio should promise a fixed number of leads. The goal is to improve clarity, trust, usability and conversion paths; business results also depend on traffic, offer, pricing, reputation and follow-up.

What if I do not know which plan fits?

Use the website test above or choose “Help me choose.” We can recommend the smallest plan that actually solves the problem rather than pushing the most expensive option.
