Clear terms beat persuasive tricks.

Do I own the website?+

With a one-time makeover, ownership and handoff are defined in the project scope. Membership pricing assumes an ongoing managed relationship; ownership/transfer terms are agreed before work starts.

What does “updates included” actually mean?+

Each membership has a defined update allowance. Small content changes are included; new pages, new systems or large redesigns are scoped separately so neither side gets surprised.

What about hosting and SSL?+

Membership plans include managed hosting and HTTPS/SSL. One-time projects can include deployment and a separate maintenance arrangement if you want us to keep managing it.

Can you guarantee leads or sales?+

No. A strong website can remove friction, improve clarity and support conversion, but traffic, offer quality, reputation and sales follow-up all matter. We will not invent performance claims.
