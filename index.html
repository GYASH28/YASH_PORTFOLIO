How long does a website usually take?+

Smaller builds can move quickly once content and direction are clear. Larger catalogues or custom experiences take longer. We agree the scope and realistic timeline before work begins.

Do I own the website?+

On one-time plans, yes—the finished build is yours. Managed memberships are designed for businesses that prefer YKG Digital to keep hosting, updating and caring for the site.

Can you improve my existing website?+

Yes. If the current foundation is worth saving, we can improve it. If rebuilding is the smarter route, we'll tell you rather than selling patches forever.

Do you handle hosting and updates?+

Yes on managed plans. Hosting, HTTPS/SSL, monitoring and agreed updates are included based on the membership you choose.
