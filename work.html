01 / FAKHRIMART

Yarn without
the maze.

FakhriMart is a wholesale yarn business with a catalogue large enough to become a usability problem. The website had to feel premium without making discovery slower.

CLIENTFakhriMart / Fakhri Yarns
TYPEWholesale catalogue
ROLEDesign + build
LIVEOpen website ↗

THE PROBLEM

A large range can feel like no direction.

Wholesale customers often arrive knowing a material, use case, colour family or yarn type — but not the exact product name. A site that simply dumps products on screen pushes the sorting work back onto the customer.

The goal was to give the catalogue a visible hierarchy: an obvious entry point, meaningful narrowing, enough comparison context and a clear route to enquiry.

THE APPROACH

Design the buying logic, not just the pages.

The experience is structured around the questions a buyer naturally asks: Where do I start? How do I narrow this? Which option fits? How do I ask about it? Does all of this still work on my phone?

That customer logic became the backbone of the homepage, catalogue, comparison, enquiry and mobile behaviour.

THE CUSTOMER JOURNEY

Don't read it.
Inspect it.

Switch between the five moments the experience was designed around.

01 / DISCOVER

Make a large catalogue feel approachable.

The first screen gives visitors a clear starting point instead of asking them to decode the entire catalogue at once.

“I know where to start.”

RESPONSIVE BY DESIGN

Mobile isn't the desktop squeezed smaller.

The mobile composition changes the priority and spacing of the interface while preserving the same buying logic. The customer should still understand where to start, what to inspect and how to enquire.

WHAT THIS PROJECT PROVES

Premium does not have to mean complicated.

The strongest improvement is not a specific animation or component. It is that a customer can move through a large product range with less uncertainty.

That is the standard YKG Digital is aiming for: visual quality that earns attention, structure that earns trust and interaction that helps rather than performs.
