## Style Prompt

YKG Cinematic Editorial: a human, evidence-led personal portfolio film. Use near-black frames, warm bone light, a restrained electric-cyan signal, casual portraiture, film-grain texture, and deliberate camera-like movement. The mood is intimate, composed, and quietly ambitious rather than futuristic or corporate.

## Colors

- `#090A0A` — deep charcoal canvas
- `#F2EADB` — warm bone type and highlight
- `#00B9DF` — electric-cyan signal accent
- `#6B4C2D` — low warm amber bloom
- `#17252A` — muted blue-black shadow

## Typography

- Barlow Condensed — large display and direct statements
- Recursive Mono — quiet labels, metadata, and technical cues

## What NOT to Do

- No generic AI dashboards, holograms, or neon sci-fi visuals.
- No corporate stock imagery, suits, or fabricated success metrics.
- No oversized block-type title cards that fill the entire screen.
- No frantic motion, jump cuts, or gratuitous visual effects.
